Appendix

A short list of some signs in the Heavens I’ve been watching at the time of this writing.

  1. Sept 2021 (Rosh Hashanah): Supernova in Cetus (The Sea Monster)
    The initialization point of the system clock. The "time, times, and half a time" (42-month) baseline countdown begins.
  2. March 14, 2025: Deep-Time Core Collapse (GRB 250314A)
    Detected at a record-breaking redshift of z ≈ 7.3. High-energy light originating from the absolute dawn of creation hits the grid, terminating the 42-month countdown.
  3. June 29, 2025: Supernova SN 2025pht in Eridanus (The Celestial River)
    The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) pulls back the layer to isolate the red supergiant progenitor star image immediately before its explosion.
  4. July 1, 2025: Interstellar Object 3I/ATLAS
    The third confirmed interstellar traveler enters the solar system with no visible comet tail, executing its trajectory in absolute stealth.
  5. July 2, 2025: Record Ultra-Long Burst (GRB 250702B)
    Shatters all cosmic records by broadcasting a continuous high-energy pulse for over 7 hours straight.
  6. July 2025: Supernova SN 2025rbs
    An exceptionally bright Type Ia explosion lights up Pegasus (The White Horse) right after the 7-hour burst finishes.
  7. August 18, 2025: Shock-Cooling Burst (AT2025ulz) in Serpens Caput (Head of the Snake)
    An exotic, ultra-young cosmic flash detonates directly inside the mouth coordinates of the serpent constellation.
  8. Late 2025: Failed Supernova in Sagittarius (The Archer)
    A massive star collapses directly into a black hole, winking out of physical sight with zero outward fire.
  9. March 17, 2026: Cosmic Volcano (GRB 260317A)
    A massive, high-energy gamma-ray burst blows open the coordinate grid directly inside Draco (The Dragon).
  10. April 22, 2026 (Earth Day): Twin Supernovae (SN 2026gkg / SN 2026gkh)
    Two independent stars undergo simultaneous explosions inside Draco, creating a dual cosmic warning.
  11. June 2026: Binary Sibling Remnants (IC 443 / G189.6+3.3)
    NASA isolates overlapping supernova remnants inside Gemini (The Twins), proving two stars from a single binary system exploded sequentially.
  12. June 7, 2026: Over-Luminous Surge (SN 2026odr) in the Phoenix Wing
    An exotic, abnormally bright Type Ia-91T supernova flashes directly adjacent to Gamma Phoenicis, marking the outer edge of the Phoenix's left wing.
  13. June 24, 2026: Divergent Initializations in Volans (The Flying Fish)
    Discovery of two wildly opposite, massive planetary types initializing inside the southern sky grid.
  14. June 25, 2026: The Davidic Light Cipher (T Coronae Borealis / The Blaze Star)
    Reaches its peak calculation window. The "Sign of the Son of Man" remains dim on the front-end user interface because the manual conditions for human return were not checked off by the test subjects.
