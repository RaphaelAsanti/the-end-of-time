The Three States of Light

Light exists in three states. The first is the wave‑state, the light you see from a lightbulb or the sun. The second is the particle‑state, the photon form described by quantum mechanics. The third is the solid‑state, discovered by mankind only recently, in which light can organize itself into stable, structured, solid‑like formations. Modern researchers have demonstrated that under engineered conditions, light can behave as a coherent solid, forming ordered patterns and mass‑like structures. This discovery establishes the scientific foundation for discussing lightbodies: bodies composed not of biological tissue, but of coherent, structured light.

Unlike water, which has three states (solid, liquid, and vapor) and requires time, temperature, and energy exchange to transition between them, light does not need any warming, cooling, melting, or evaporation. Light can shift between its three states—wave, particle, and solid—instantaneously. This is because light is not bound by thermal inertia or phase‑change latency. When conditions change, light reorganizes itself immediately, without delay. This property is essential for understanding coherent solid‑light lightbodies.

1. The Lightbody as Coherent Solid Light

Our true bodies are made of coherent solid light. They are humanlike in form, permanently mature, and unchanging. Everyone appears in their late twenties or early thirties, the point of perfect strength, clarity, and beauty. Every person is unique. Men are men and women are women, because gender is encoded in the soul itself.

Lightbodies do not age, do not decay, do not get sick, do not feel pain, do not get injured, and do not die. There are no organs, no fluids, no biological systems, and no biological processes of any kind. Nothing can harm a lightbody. Nothing can damage it. Nothing can diminish it.

Hair is solid light, hairlike in appearance and texture, and can be restyled instantly by running the fingers through it in a particular way.

2. Clothing as Part of the Body

Clothing is solid light shaped to look and feel like fabric, but it is inseparable from the body. It is part of the lightbody’s outer structure and cannot be removed, torn, damaged, or separated. Nakedness is impossible because the covering is built into the form itself.

All clothing is designed by God Almighty Himself. Every garment, every style, every detail comes directly from Him. His fashion sense is far beyond anything humans can imagine. Human clothing is limited by materials, sewing, trends, and personal taste. His designs are perfect in color, shape, fit, and beauty. Nothing clashes. Nothing wears out. Nothing looks awkward. Every garment suits the person exactly, because He knows their soul completely and designs for who they truly are and what they like as an individual.

Lightbody clothing is not a separate object. It is the outer layer of the body arranged into a particular design. Changing clothing does not involve putting something on or taking something off. Instead, the lightbody reshapes its outer layer into a new design instantly.

Closets contain representations of clothing, not physical garments. A representation is a solid‑light model of the clothing design a person wants to wear. It shows the appearance and texture of the garment exactly as God created it. When a person touches a representation, the lightbody reads the design and immediately reforms its outer layer to match it. The representation itself does not become the clothing; it simply displays the design that the body will adopt.

Every representation a person has ever chosen remains available forever, and new ones can be added at any time. The closet is not storage space for clothes. It is a gallery of God‑designed garments — a collection of solid‑light representations that the lightbody can instantly copy onto itself.

Because every garment is designed by God Almighty Himself, and because each one is formed from the solid‑light structure of the body, clothing is never separate from us. It is part of our form. When we select a change of clothing, the lightbody simply reshapes its outer layer into the new design. There is no moment of transition, no gap, and no exposure. The covering is continuous and permanent. For this reason, we never see ourselves naked. Naked simply isn’t an option for us.

3. Eating for Enjoyment and Socialization

Lightbodies do eat, but not for energy, survival, or biological function. Eating is purely for enjoyment and socialization. Food does not metabolize. It dissipates inside the body and disappears. There is no digestion, no energy expenditure, and no waste.

Meals exist because they are pleasurable, communal, relational, and part of shared life. Eating is an act of delight and companionship, not biology. Because lightbodies have no biological systems, they also have no reproductive organs and no biological sex functions.

4. The Distributed Mind and Its Capacity

Lightbodies are not limited by a flesh brain. The entire body functions as the mind. Information is stored and delivered instantly throughout the structure. The soul contains identity and complete personal history. The lightbody acts as the active processor of knowledge.

As metaphor, the soul is the hard drive, and the lightbody is both the RAM and the processor. The internal photonic capacity of the lightbody is not our identity store and not our long-term memory. It is simply our RAM — a workspace capable of holding more information than entire civilizations.

I have certain musical abilities in my soul. I do not play a musical instrument here, in my flesh body. My flesh mind never bothered to download the knowledge, and I never bothered to develop the muscle memory that biological practice requires. When I am home, in my lightbody, I play my guitar. My soul contains the full knowledge. My lightbody has the ability. The knowledge is stored directly in the fingertips themselves, and they can move at the speed of light. Skills are not learned; they are present. The flesh mind is terribly inefficient by comparison.

Even though we all have the same type of lightbody, each of us has our own individual likes, dislikes, preferences, memories, and skills. My soul contains experiences that are uniquely mine, and every other person’s soul contains experiences uniquely theirs. We learn from each other directly, by talking to each other in social situations. Knowledge is exchanged instantly and perfectly, not through books or devices, but through conversation itself. Every individual carries their entire history, and sharing it is effortless.

Because we remember everything perfectly, we have no need for written language. There is no forgetting, no loss of detail, no decay of memory. Every experience, every piece of knowledge, every moment is permanently accessible through the soul and instantly available to the lightbody.

This perfect recall eliminates the need for libraries, books, archives, technology, or the internet. We do not store information externally. We do not retrieve information from devices. We do not depend on tools to remember or learn. All knowledge is internal, permanent, and instantly accessible.

If a lightbody were used purely as a storage medium, a six-foot, two-hundred-pound human figure contains enough mass-energy to store on the order of 10³² to 10³³ bytes. This is hundreds of billions of trillions of times more information than all data stored by humanity.

A lightbody could hold every book, every video, every file, every memory, every experience, every scientific record, and still remain almost entirely empty. Its capacity is effectively limitless for any purpose a person could ever need.

But we never use this capacity. We do not need to. We only have one connection — our direct link to God Almighty — and WE ONLY HAVE ONE. IT’S ALL WE NEED OR WANT.

A lightbody is not “smarter” than an AI. A lightbody is categorically beyond the concept of intelligence itself. An AI is a finite machine that guesses. It relies on limited hardware, limited storage, limited training data, limited accuracy, and limited reliability. It cannot generate perfect knowledge, cannot correct itself perfectly, and cannot access all information.

A lightbody does not guess. It does not infer. It does not compute. It does not rely on stored data. It operates through direct connection to God Almighty, receiving perfect knowledge instantly. The lightbody’s photonic structure is only the interface — the conduit — not the source. The difference between a lightbody and an AI is the difference between infinity and a calculator, between omniscience and a machine that approximates.

5. Instantaneous Travel

Lightbodies do not move through space. They transform instantly into particles, relocate anywhere in the universe, and reform as solid light. No time passes. No distance is experienced. There is no motion. Teleportation stations do not exist; they would serve no purpose.

The entire universe is functionally outside the front door. One does not need to leave the living room to appear anywhere.

6. Direct Individual Connection to God Almighty

Every person has a direct, individual connection to God Almighty. This connection is the operating principle of the lightbody. Without Him, the lightbody cannot function.

WE ONLY HAVE ONE. IT’S ALL WE NEED OR WANT.

He sustains the form, provides knowledge, and handles travel. Because He supplies everything directly, there is no internet, no libraries, no books, and no written language. When someone wishes to learn something, they simply let Him know, and He transfers the knowledge instantly.

7. Sleep as Private Time With God

Lightbodies never tire, but we do sleep by choice. Sleep is not biological rest, and we do not dream as flesh minds do. Sleep is our private, individual time with God Almighty. Whenever a person chooses to sleep, He meets with them directly. This is not symbolic. It is literal.

When I sleep next to my eternal paradise companion, we are physically together, yet each of us is alone with God, who is alone with both of us simultaneously. Physical closeness and divine intimacy operate on different layers. Our bodies remain next to each other, but our souls are each in direct audience with Him.

8. Summary

  • No aging, sickness, pain, injury, or death
  • Clothing inseparable from the body
  • Eating for enjoyment and socialization
  • Distributed mind
  • Instantaneous travel
  • Direct connection to God — only one, all we need or want
  • Sleep as private time with God

10. Human Identity and Human Experience

Even with a lightbody, we are still human. Our souls are human souls, and our experiences are human experiences. The lightbody does not replace our humanity; it simply provides a far superior suit for our souls to interact with each other and with the creation itself.

The soul remains the true individual. The lightbody is the interface — the perfected, coherent solid-light form through which we perceive, act, communicate, and relate. It is not who we are. It is the ideal instrument for who we are.

In the flesh, our bodies are fragile, temporary, and inefficient. In the lightbody, our humanity is expressed without limitation, without decay, without pain, and without death. We are still ourselves, only finally equipped with the form originally intended for us.
