Chapter 4.1 – Lightbodies

1. The Lightbody as Coherent Solid Light

Our true bodies are made of coherent solid light. They are humanlike in form, permanently mature, and unchanging. Everyone appears in their late twenties or early thirties, the point of perfect strength, clarity, and beauty. Every person is unique. Men are men and women are women, because gender is encoded in the soul itself.

Lightbodies do not age, do not decay, do not get sick, do not feel pain, do not get injured, and do not die. There are no organs, no fluids, no biological systems, and no biological processes of any kind.

2. Clothing as Part of the Body

Clothing is solid light shaped to look and feel like fabric, but it is inseparable from the body. It cannot be removed. Nakedness is impossible. Changing clothing is instantaneous re-patterning.

3. Eating for Enjoyment and Socialization

Lightbodies eat purely for enjoyment and socialization. Food dissipates inside the body and disappears. There is no digestion, no metabolism, and no waste.

4. The Distributed Mind

Lightbodies have no brain. The entire body functions as the mind. The soul contains identity and history. The lightbody acts as the processor, storing skills directly in its structure.

5. Instantaneous Travel

Lightbodies transform into particles, relocate instantly anywhere in the universe, and reform as solid light. No time passes. No distance is experienced. Teleportation stations do not exist.

6. Direct Individual Connection to God Almighty

Every person has a direct, individual connection to God Almighty. This connection is the operating principle of the lightbody. Without Him, the lightbody cannot function.

WE ONLY HAVE ONE. IT’S ALL WE NEED OR WANT.

He sustains the form, provides knowledge, and handles travel. Because He supplies everything directly, there is no internet, no libraries, no books, and no written language.

7. Sleep as Private Time With God

Lightbodies never tire, but we sleep by choice. Sleep is not biological rest. It is private, individual time with God Almighty. When two companions sleep beside each other, they remain physically together while each is alone with God, who is alone with both simultaneously.

8. Summary

  • Coherent solid light
  • No aging, sickness, pain, injury, or death
  • Clothing inseparable from the body
  • Eating for enjoyment and socialization
  • Distributed mind
  • Instantaneous travel
  • Direct connection to God — only one, all we need or want
  • Sleep as private time with God
