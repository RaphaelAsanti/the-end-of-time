To understand how the world moved forward after the Fall, we must briefly look at Noah. Frankly, in the grand design of history, the historical figure of Noah is largely irrelevant. The Creator sealed that specific era with a visual promise—the rainbow—guaranteeing that He would never destroy the entire earth with water again. However, standard Sunday school narratives bypass the deeper lessons of this event. Examining what is usually left untaught helps us see exactly how the universe operates when human behavior becomes completely corrupt.

The 1656-Year Free-Fall

The generations between Adam and the flood completely severed their connection to God. Left with no behavioral boundaries, humanity fell into an absolute, lawless free-for-all. Because their deep thoughts and words were entirely rotten, the initial rollout of human civilization collapsed.

The world simply could not sustain the sheer volume of human errors. This massive moral failure is why the ancient world was so short-lived. It lasted exactly 1,656 years and 10 generations from the creation of Adam to the execution of the flood. 10 is the number of “completion”.

The Enoch Exception

For those who want to follow a fascinating side story, consider Enoch. Very little is written about this man in the standard scriptures except for a single, mysterious sentence: "Enoch walked faithfully with God; then he was no more, because God took him" (Genesis 5:24). He did not die a natural death. His spirit was so pure that he bypassed physical decay entirely. Because of this sudden disappearance, some ancient traditions believe he was transformed directly into an angel.

Enoch’s timeline is mathematically precise. He lived exactly three and a half generations before Noah. This placement is critical because scripture states that God visits the sins of the fathers upon the third and fourth generations (Exodus 20:5). Enoch stood right at that generational tipping point, just before the full weight of human wickedness collapsed the ancient world.

Furthermore, Enoch lived for exactly 365 years. This number matches a full solar cycle. A solar year is built on 365 daily cycles wrapped inside four distinct seasonal cycles. Enoch's life span serves as a physical layout for this reality, showing that the universe operates on precise cycles within cycles.

Before he vanished, he left behind a text known as the Book of Enoch. This text describes many of the deep wrongdoings occurring on Earth at that time. It does not fit cleanly alongside the Torah, the Prophets, or the New Testament, which is why it was excluded from the Bible. However, it still exists as a historical record, and any curious reader can easily find it and read it today.

DNA Pairs and the Meaning of the Ark

Mainstream literalists obsess over the physical size of the Ark. They argue about how millions of animal species could fit into a single wooden vessel. Let us use our intellectual honesty: the Ark was not large enough to carry that many physical animals.

The literal text hides a much deeper truth about biological reality:

  • The 2x2 Pattern: The command to bring animals "two by two" was a symbolic blueprint mapping out DNA base pairs. The Ark was designed to preserve the pure, uncorrupted building blocks of physical life.
  • The Vehicle: In ancient scriptural language, a "boat" or "ark" does not just mean a wooden box. It represents a spiritual means of travel—a localized, protected environment built to transport life safely through a hostile world.

This mathematical signature is hidden directly inside the measurements of the vessel. The Ark was designed to be 300 cubits long and 50 cubits wide. In the ancient Hebrew alphabet system, 300 corresponds to the letter Shin, which signifies the raw Ruach—the active, moving Spirit of God. 50 corresponds to the letter Nun, which literally means a fish moving through water, signifying life and growth.

Combined, the physical measurements of the boat reveal its true meaning: The Spirit of God propagating life through the fluid world.

The Purge Pattern: From Water to Fire

Immediately after the Ark made land and the earth was restored, Noah took clean animals and completely burned them on an altar. This physical action serves as an advanced warning sign for the end of time. The sacrificial smoke declared a hard law of our universe: although nature was salvaged by water that time, nature will absolutely not be saved the next time.

The prophets consistently record that the final shutdown of this world will be executed by a total destruction by fire. Centuries later, John the Baptist highlighted this exact shift in the timeline:

"I indeed baptize you with water... but He who is coming after me... will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire." (Matthew 3:11)

Water was the temporary cleansing; Fire is the final structural completion.

The Backwards Walk of Betrayal

Once the emergency reset was finalized, humanity immediately defaulted back to its compromised instincts. When Noah lay exposed inside his tent, Ham and his brothers "walked in backward" to cover him (Genesis 9:23).

In the language of scriptural actions, walking backward is a profound physical symbol. It symbolizes calculated betrayal and a split mind. It represents pretending to honor a boundary while actively turning your back on the truth.

The Split Inheritance

This immediate post-flood defiance shattered the family structure of the new world. Of the three sons who stepped off the vehicle to repopulate the earth, two-thirds of them were compromised by curses.

  • Shem—whose name literally translates to "The Name" or "Truth"—stood as the only ancestor to leave the encounter entirely free of a curse.

Through this massive historical bottleneck, a fractured human race emerged: one tiny thread holding onto Truth, while the remaining majority carried the encoded heritage of defiance.

Rebuilding Babylon

Unsurprisingly, this new beginning concluded with humanity executing the exact same error they committed before the purge. Ham’s cursed son Nimrod (whose name means ‘rebel’) immediately set to work rebuilding Babylon—the ultimate monument to human pride and the delusional desire to rule without God.

They wanted to build a tower that reached heaven, repeating Satan's original mental trap of trying to sit in the center seat. They failed then, but the structure of that rebellion never truly left our world. Like it or not, Babylon is exactly where every single human being is living today. The global society around you is simply that same ancient, arrogant system running on modernized hardware.

The Ouroboros Effect

The word Ouroboros (also spelled uroboros) originates from the Ancient Greek language. It is a compound word combining two distinct parts:

  • οὐρά (ourá): Meaning "tail".
  • -βόρος (-bóros): Meaning "-devouring" or "-eating". This comes from the Greek verb βιβρώσκω (bibrṓskō), which means "to eat up" or "to devour".

When combined, the word οὐροβόρος (ourobóros) literally translates to "tail-devouring" or "tail-eater".

Historical Evolution

  • The Image is Older Than the Word: While the name we use is Greek, the earliest recorded visual representation of the symbol actually dates back to Ancient Egypt (around the 14th century BCE). It was famously found inscribed in the tomb of King Tutankhamun to signify the cyclical nature of time and the boundaries of the universe.
  • The Greek Adoption: The Greeks adopted the Egyptian iconography into their own magical, Gnostic, and alchemical traditions. They gave it the name Ouroboros and used it to encapsulate the famous philosophical phrase, "Hen to Pan"—meaning "The All is One"
  • The Ouroboros (The Circle): Traditionally, a snake consuming its own tail represents eternity, the cyclical nature of the universe, and the concept of endless return. It signifies that life, death, and rebirth are all part of an interconnected, never-ending loop where endings are just new beginnings where the snake (satan) attempts to perpetuate his existence with a never ending story.
  • The Figure-Eight (Infinity): When the snake twists into a figure-eight, it directly mirrors the mathematical symbol for infinity (∞). This shape amplifies the traditional meaning, visually tracking an infinite journey that crosses over itself but never truly stops.

Essentially, the figure-eight Ouroboros is the ultimate visual metaphor for the snake (satan) attempting to perpetualize him self via infinite regeneration, eternal time, and the balance of opposing forces of death and life within a unified whole.

With the backwards betrayal of Noah that resulted and the rebuilding of Babylon by ‘The Rebel’, the snake got to eat his own tail in a figure 8 that time. We see in Revelation that he will try again:

Then from the mouth of the serpent spewed water like a river to overtake the woman and sweep her away in the torrent. But the earth came to the help of the woman, and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed the river that the dragon had poured from his mouth. And the dragon was enraged at the woman, and went to make war with the rest of her children, who keep the commandments of God and hold to the testimony of Jesus. – Revelation 12:15-17

But the Rainbow in the sky was God’s Command saying “Never Again”.
