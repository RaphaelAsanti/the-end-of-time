Chapter 5: What is a Human?

A soul in tension that's learning to fly
Condition grounded, but determined to try

Learning to Fly – Pink Floyd

You are not a body. You are a soul. Your soul is the real, permanent you. It is a living recording device. Every single word you have ever spoken and every deed you have ever done is saved on your soul. Your soul is your true identity as an individual.

Your physical body is just an interface. It is like an avatar or a spacesuit. Whether your body is made of light, flesh, or cardboard and Styrofoam, it does not matter. The body is just the machine you use to touch, experience, and interact with this physical world.

Your mind is the bridge. It connects your heavy physical body to your spiritual soul. Your subconscious mind is the gateway between the two. Science actually proves how slow our physical bodies are. Recent brain studies show that your subconscious flesh-mind takes up to seven full seconds to process a decision before you even realize it. That lag happens because your physical brain is slow, but your soul is instantaneous.

Your soul is never standing still. It is in a constant state of flux. It moves back and forth on a spectrum between totally pure and completely putrid. Good and righteous deeds, as defined by God, purify your soul. They make it clean and light. Unrighteous words and bad deeds do the opposite. They putrefy your soul, making it rot.

Jesus explained this directly to Peter. He said that a person is not ruined by the food he eats. Food only affects the body. What harms a person is the words that come out of his mouth. Those words come from the soul. They reveal what is inside the soul. In the same way, a person’s actions show the true state of their inner self.

This creates a permanent feedback loop. As your soul becomes more pure or more putrid, it changes how you naturally act. If your soul is rotting, unrighteous behavior becomes your default setting. If your soul is pure, righteous actions become your natural state. You act out what you are, because your soul is who you are.

In the end, the physical creation will vanish. The meatsuits will be stripped away. God will not judge your earthly looks, your wealth, or your physical status. God judges souls. He will look at the permanent ledger of who you chose to become while you were here.

⭐ The 7-Second Meatsuit Lag

In 2008, neuroscientists at the Max Planck Institute discovered something mind-boggling. They put volunteers into brain scanners and told them to push a button whenever they felt like it. The scanners revealed that the brain's prefrontal cortex—the part of the brain that handles conscious, free-will decisions—was actively preparing the choice up to 7 full seconds before the person even realized they had made it.

Mainstream science claims this proves free will is fake, arguing your brain is just a pre-programmed machine. But this book views it through the lens of the "meatsuit lag"

The Soul's Choice: Your soul makes an instantaneous choice at spiritual lightspeed.

The Cortex Processor: The cerebral cortex acts like a physical computer monitor. It receives that pure soul signal and begins processing the data.

The Material Delay: Because flesh and neurons move much slower than spirit, it takes the cerebral cortex seven full seconds to translate the soul's decision into physical matter.

The brain scan isn't catching your brain making a random choice; it is catching your physical processor slowly booting up to execute your soul's command.

Righteousness vs. Perfection

This massive data gap between our slow, heavy flesh and our high-speed soul signature explains the ultimate paradox of scripture. On one hand, the Apostle Paul looks at our broken state and declares a hard truth:

"There is no one righteous, not even one." — Romans 3:10

Inside this 40-meat suited flesh mind, your physical actions will always glitch. The flesh is hard-wired with survival instincts, fear, and friction. It is physically impossible for a human crawling through a fallen material world to achieve absolute, error-free righteousness on their own. No one is capable of actually perfecting themselves.

"for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."— Romans 3:23

Yet, Jesus delivers a command that seems completely impossible to fulfill:

"Be perfect, therefore, as your heavenly Father is perfect." — Matthew 5:48

The solution to this impossible problem belongs exclusively to the Creator. Because we cannot achieve perfection on our own, God exercises His absolute power to erase and nullify your deeds. This divine data wipe is what true forgiveness actually is. By clearing the negative entries, He grants you your righteousness and completes the purification process for you.

We see a brilliant biblical example of this instant data clearing when Moses meets God at the burning bush. Moses was a runaway fugitive who had committed a murder in Egypt. Yet, when God commands him to put his hand into his cloak and pull it out, it is instantly made white and clean. God simply nullified the murder in the record of his soul. In a single moment, the past slate was wiped away, and Moses was purified for his mission. The Qur’an explicitly confirms this exact system rule for believers:

"He will erase their evil deeds and improve their state." — Qur’an 47:2

The Dangerous Inverse: The Final Nullification

However, the system rules run both ways, and the inverse of this law is terrifyingly true. Just as God can erase your bad deeds to purify you, He can also nullify your good deeds and completely putrefy your soul at the final judgment if He chooses. This is the exact warning Jesus delivered to those who relied on their own resume of physical actions rather than true submission:

"Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter the kingdom of heaven, but only the one who does the will of my Father who is in heaven. Many will say to me on that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name and in your name drive out demons and, in your name, perform many miracles?' Then I will tell them plainly, 'I never knew you. Away from me, you evildoers!'" — Matthew 7:21-23

This passage describes the absolute nullification of good deeds. These individuals had a massive checklist of outward actions, but because their inner code was not aligned with the Father's will, God deleted their entire record. When the Creator invokes this system wipe, your past works are treated as if they never existed, leaving the soul completely bankrupt. Whether your data is purified or deleted depends entirely on the sovereign choice of the Source at the final deadline.

The Meaning of Life: Passing the Incubator Test

When you strip away all the noise of the physical world, the ultimate meaning of human life becomes incredibly simple and clear. The purpose of your existence is to make your soul righteous so you can pass the final judgment.

You are not here to chase comfort, build an earthly legacy, or collect temporary material wealth. This entire physical creation is not a permanent home. It is a temporary, high-friction incubator for human souls to evolve or devolve. God engineered this universe with heavy limitations, time loops, and physical suffering for one specific reason: to act as a cosmic testing ground. The friction forces you to make real, free-will choices under pressure, and those choices are continuously recorded to your soul signature at a rate of 40 million bits per second. Every single human soul who has ever crawled through this timeline will either pass or fail at the end.

You cannot pass this test by relying on your own broken, 40-bit animal chassis. If you try to stand before the perfect Source on your own merit, your deeds will be nullified. To make your soul righteous, you must completely surrender your will to the Creator, aligning your underlying code with His absolute perfection so that He chooses to erase your glitches and grant you His purity.

When the incubator reaches its hard scheduled deadline, this entire material matrix will be cleanly dismantled. There are no gray areas and no third options. Your body is just a temporary uniform; passing the test of the incubator is the only thing that matters for eternity.
