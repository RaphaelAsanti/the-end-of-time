The End of Time

by Raphael Asanti

The End of Time explores the human search for meaning at the intersection of theology and quantum theory. Many readers arrive here asking whether modern physics changes how we understand God, creation, and the meaning of life.

  • The collapse of possibilities and the nature of divine decision
  • Time, consciousness, and the structure of creation
  • Reconciling Abrahamic theology with a probabilistic universe

About the Author

Raphael Asanti writes at the intersection of theology, consciousness, and the structure of reality. His work focuses on how ancient metaphysical questions can be re-examined through the lens of modern physics, especially quantum theory. He approaches these subjects from a conceptual and philosophical perspective, aiming to make complex ideas accessible without oversimplifying them.

Reader Impressions

“A rare work that treats both physics and theology with seriousness. It made me rethink how consciousness fits into the structure of reality.”
“Clear, thoughtful, and conceptually rigorous. The book avoids hype and gets straight to the philosophical core of quantum theory.”

Rather than using quantum mechanics as a slogan or a proof, this work examines how uncertainty, superposition, and the collapse of possibilities mirror theological questions about divine will, freedom, and destiny. It asks how consciousness, time, and existence can be understood when both Scripture and physics are taken seriously.

Readers interested in the meaning of life in a universe described by quantum physics — and in how Abrahamic theological concepts engage that picture of reality — will find a direct conceptual treatment of those themes.
